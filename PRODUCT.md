# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Auth.js, Zod, React Hook Form, and shadcn/ui as specified in the supplied Day 1 brief. The brief delegated implementation details within that stack.

## Users
Independent IELTS learners preparing for an exam and looking for structured practice and a clear view of their own progress.

## Product Purpose
Help people prepare for IELTS with structured lessons, realistic practice, and personal progress tracking. Day 1 succeeds when a learner can register, sign in, and reach a dashboard tied to their account.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Day 1 includes a public landing page, account registration and login, protected dashboard, and empty states for learning features that are not implemented yet. Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking are the product's section names. No learning activity or outcome data is available yet.

## Brand Commitments
The product is named Bandwise and uses the supplied Bandwise logo. The supplied brief specifies a white background, deep plum text (#24213D), muted secondary text (#666580), restrained violet (#7047FF) and cyan (#00B8D9) accents, and light borders (#E8E8F0). The first ClassDojo screenshot is a reference for general design principles, not a template to copy.

## Evidence on Hand
The user supplied a Day 1 implementation brief. There are no real testimonials, learner outcomes, user counts, practice statistics, or activity records to publish. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles
- Make IELTS preparation feel clear and approachable.
- Connect each learner to their own account and dashboard.
- Show honest empty states until learning data exists.
- Keep the four IELTS skills easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, useful validation and error messages, and responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
