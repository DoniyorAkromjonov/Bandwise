# Bandwise Design System

## Direction
An editorial study companion: IELTS preparation presented like a clear, well-made workbook. The page pairs confident editorial typography with a practical four-skill index. It preserves the supplied clean white palette and uses color to distinguish skill areas and navigation states.

## Palette
- Canvas: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#24213D`
- Secondary text: `#666580`
- Violet action: `#7047FF`
- Cyan accent: `#00B8D9`
- Border: `#E8E8F0`
- Quiet surface: `#FAFAFC`

## Type
- Display: Fraunces, with tight but readable editorial leading.
- Interface and body: DM Sans.
- Keep headings compact and body copy easy to scan.

## Components
- White surfaces, fine borders, and restrained soft shadows.
- Card radii generally between 16 and 24px; buttons between 12 and 16px.
- Violet is reserved for primary actions and active emphasis.
- Skill accents use pale cyan, violet, warm sand, and green surfaces with darker readable icon colors.
- Controls use visible focus rings, hover feedback, and clear disabled and error states.

## Interaction and layout
- Landing page uses a spacious split hero with a four-skill study map, followed by features, IELTS skills, steps, and a final call to action.
- On small screens, navigation becomes a keyboard-operable expandable menu and content stacks in a single column.
- Dashboard content is private and session-backed. Empty states stand in for data that the platform does not yet collect.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid color as the only way to distinguish a state.
